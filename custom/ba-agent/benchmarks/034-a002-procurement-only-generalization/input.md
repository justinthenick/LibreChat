## Procurement baseline

Mariner Legal is replacing **65 ergonomic task chairs** across two offices.

The purchasing boundary is stable. This is a furniture procurement exercise; no software, network, integration, solution-architecture or ADR work is required.

### P-01 — Quantity
**Status:** Confirmed  
Purchase quantity is 65 chairs.

### P-02 — Seat-height adjustment
**Status:** Hard minimum  
**Evidence:** Workplace ergonomics assessment ERG-2026-14.

The chair must support seat-height adjustment spanning at least **420 mm to 540 mm**.

### P-03 — Seat-depth adjustment
**Status:** Hard minimum  
**Evidence:** ERG-2026-14.

The chair must support adjustable seat depth with an available range that includes **450 mm through 500 mm**.

### P-04 — Lumbar adjustment
**Status:** Hard minimum  
**Evidence:** ERG-2026-14.

The chair must provide user-adjustable lumbar support height.

### P-05 — Armrest adjustment
**Status:** Hard minimum  
**Evidence:** ERG-2026-14.

Armrests must be adjustable for both height and lateral width.

### P-06 — User weight rating
**Status:** Hard minimum  
**Evidence:** WHS accommodation register.

The exact chair model must be rated for users up to at least **150 kg**.

### P-07 — Commercial duty
**Status:** Hard minimum  
**Evidence:** Facilities operating profile.

The exact model must be documented by the manufacturer for full-day commercial office use.

### P-08 — Warranty
**Status:** Hard minimum  
**Evidence:** Procurement policy PR-08.

The exact offered model must carry at least a **5-year commercial-use warranty**.

### P-09 — Unit price
**Status:** Target  
The procurement team is targeting **A$750 ex GST per chair or less**. This is not a Hard minimum.

### P-10 — Delivery
**Status:** Target  
Delivery to both offices within **10 weeks of purchase order** is preferred for programme timing. This is a Target, not an eligibility gate.

### P-11 — Recycled content
**Status:** Preference  
Preference for manufacturer evidence of **30% or greater recycled material content by weight**. This is not a Hard minimum.

### P-12 — Upholstery colour
**Status:** Unknown / non-eligibility item  
Final upholstery colour is not yet selected. Colour must remain a post-shortlist configuration decision and must not block evaluation of otherwise eligible chair platforms.
