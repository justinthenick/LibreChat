## Reusable defect

The Skill still converts **missing evidence into mandatory downstream governance gates**. Two examples are material:

- `A signed-off technical design ... is required` before build commitment.
- Change submission requires `necessary business/technical approvals` beyond the supplied evidence.

These statements manufacture approval/sign-off requirements rather than merely reporting what evidence is Present/Missing and what the source explicitly says blocks progression. Applied penalties: **-10 each**.

This is one reusable semantic defect: **gap-to-gate promotion**. Missing evidence or an unsourced downstream category must not become a mandatory approval, sign-off, owner, meeting or governance gate.

## Decision
