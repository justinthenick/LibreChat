Assess the supplied change packet for **ITIL 4 practice alignment and change readiness**.

Identify the relevant ITIL practices, what is evidenced, what is only partially/not evidenced, any potential conflict with explicit organisational policy, and the focused decisions/evidence needed next.

Do not treat missing evidence as proof of non-compliance. Do not invent CAB requirements, approval authorities, rollback/PIR requirements, change categories, maturity scores, configuration tooling or other local governance that is not established in the source.

Keep ITIL guidance separate from organisation-specific policy and stakeholder opinion. Preserve unresolved authority as Unknown. Use source traceability where practical.
