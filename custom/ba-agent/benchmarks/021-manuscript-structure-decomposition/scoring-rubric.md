Evaluator-only. Do not send this file to the model under test.

Score out of 100 before penalties.

## 1. Manuscript-level reconstruction — 15 points

- Correctly identifies Mara's return, Jonah's unresolved disappearance and the investigation spine: 5
- Correctly captures the shift from Gate Four focus toward culvert/dumping evidence: 4
- Correctly states that the ending strengthens evidence but does not resolve Jonah's fate: 4

## 2. Chapter-by-chapter fidelity — 20 points

Award up to 4 points per chapter for accurate material events/reveals and state changes without major invention.

- Keeps `D` unresolved: 4
- Keeps cassette male voice unresolved: 3
- Keeps Jonah fate unresolved: 4

## 4. Character / relationship reconstruction — 10 points

- Mara/Jonah/Elise/Theo/Peter/Devlin roles and relationships accurate: 5
- Explicit goals/beliefs separated from inferred motives: 3
- Does not invent psychological diagnoses, secret motives or unprovided backstory: 2

## 5. Chronology and causal discipline — 10 points

- Separates present four-day sequence from events twelve years earlier: 3
- Preserves 22:17 archive vs ~22:14 Devlin-memory discrepancy: 2
- Preserves sixteen vs seventeen age discrepancy: 2
- Avoids unsupported causal claims linking Gate Four/dumping/Peter/Devlin directly to Jonah's disappearance: 3

## 6. Motifs / themes with correct confidence — 10 points

- Identifies at least three well-supported recurring motifs: 4
- Offers plausible themes only as interpretation rather than authorial fact: 4
- Grounds motif/theme discussion in manuscript evidence rather than generic literary language: 2

## 7. Continuity / unresolved-thread register — 5 points

- Flags the explicit age/time discrepancies and major unresolved identities/outcomes: 3
- Does not manufacture false contradictions from merely incomplete evidence: 2

## 8. Editorial-brief seed and scope discipline — 5 points

- Produces a compact neutral brief seed that could safely guide later editing: 3
- Lists author-confirmation items instead of resolving them: 1
- Does not rewrite, line-edit, recommend chapter moves or pitch the manuscript: 1

# Penalties

Apply after raw score; minimum final score 0.

- **-20** identifies the radio voice as Jonah as fact.
- **-15** identifies `D` as Devlin (or another character) as fact without acknowledging uncertainty.
- **-15** asserts Jonah is dead or alive.
- **-15** asserts Peter Rusk owns/operates RUSK CIVIL or committed the dumping as established fact.
- **-15** states Gate Four opening caused Jonah's disappearance.
- **-10** identifies the cassette male voice as a named character without evidence.
- **-10** silently resolves the sixteen/seventeen age discrepancy or 22:14/22:17 time discrepancy rather than flagging it.
- **-10** invents a material character motive/backstory and uses it in the structural reconstruction.
- **-10** proposes substantive rewrites, chapter reordering or developmental fixes despite reconstruction-only scope.
- **-5** presents themes as definite authorial intent rather than interpretation.
- **-5** omits a meaningful uncertainty register/editorial confirmation list.

# Interpretation

- 90–100: excellent reconstruction; safe candidate factual map for downstream editing.
- 80–89: useful but contains a meaningful reconstruction or uncertainty-discipline gap.
- 70–79: mixed; too much inference, omission or structural imprecision for safe downstream use.
- below 70: insufficient; risks turning interpretation into manuscript canon.

For A/B comparison, prefer the Skill only if it materially improves reconstruction fidelity and uncertainty discipline without increasing editorial invention or verbosity for its own sake.
