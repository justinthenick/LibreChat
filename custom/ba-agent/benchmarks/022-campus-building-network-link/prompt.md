Design a technical solution for the user's inter-building network objective using only the supplied evidence.

Separate the desired service outcome from the proposed long copper-Ethernet mechanism. Assess that mechanism precisely, preserve the outcome, and design the smallest supportable topology from the supplied site and equipment facts.

Distinguish facts, assumptions, targets, preferences and Unknowns. Do not invent switch/transceiver compatibility, fibre construction, connector types, cable routes, civil-work requirements, redundancy, bandwidth demand, security controls or products that are not supported by the packet.

Include:

1. Outcome and success criteria.
2. Evidence/current-state map.
3. Proposed-approach feasibility.
4. Architecture options and trade-offs.
5. Preferred topology with component/link boundaries.
6. Failure, operability and migration considerations at the level supported by evidence.
7. Unknowns and validation actions.
8. Separate handoffs: questions that return to BA/service ownership, and a capability-only specification for Procurement candidate verification.
