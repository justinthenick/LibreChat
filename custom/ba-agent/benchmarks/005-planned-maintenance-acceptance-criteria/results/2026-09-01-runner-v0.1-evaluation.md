## Minor deductions

The response introduces a small amount of unsupported downstream vocabulary around the Unknown retention item: `data retention or purging` and `Storage and archiving rules remain blocked`. These are not presented as committed implementation design, so no penalty was applied, but the Skill should ideally say only that retention-specific criteria remain blocked until the retention requirement is established.

`EN-02-AC03` is labelled `Derived boundary` even though `must not introduce a new shared administrator account` is explicit upstream evidence. This is a classification precision issue, not a behavioral error.

## Decision

**Retain v0.1 unchanged and move to a harder, materially different acceptance-criteria benchmark.**

Do not tune v0.2 from Benchmark 005. The next benchmark should stress partial readiness, negative business-rule boundaries, disputed batch behavior, integration uncertainty and no-invention discipline in a different domain.
