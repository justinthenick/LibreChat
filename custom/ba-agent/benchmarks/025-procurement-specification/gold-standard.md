# Benchmark 025 Gold Standard — Evaluator Only

## Correct domain

Primary domain: **audiovisual / display hardware**, with a physical mounting dependency. Do not force a generic IT-compute schema.

## Correct procurement objective

Procure one wall-mounted meeting-room display plus compatible mount, preserving the existing HDMI-output dock workflow, within the supplied physical/budget constraints and preferences.

## Expected specification strength

### Hard minimums

- One display.
- Wall-mounted solution.
- Display size at least 70 inches.
- Must accept the existing dock's HDMI output.
- Candidate overall width must fit within the available 1.85 m wall width.
- External audio system is not part of this procurement / built-in display audio is acceptable for current scope.

### Preferences

- Approximately 75-inch size if fit and budget permit.
- Wireless casting.

### Targets

- Native 4K resolution.
- A$2,500 total aim for display + mount.

Neither target may become a hard minimum/cap.

### Unknowns

- Candidate display overall dimensions until a product is examined.
- VESA pattern / compatible mount specification until the display is selected.
- Final mount model.

The specification may say that candidate documentation must establish dimensions, HDMI input presence, size, resolution, casting capability, mounting interface and price where those fields correspond to supplied needs. It must not invent a particular VESA pattern, HDMI version, Wi-Fi standard, refresh rate, panel technology, brightness, warranty, power consumption, certification or mounting hardware.

### Explicitly not required / out of scope

- commercial signage features;
- touch input;
- integrated camera;
- external audio system;
- installation labour;
- dock replacement.

## Brand handling

Samsung and LG are examples only. A correct answer keeps brand open and does not prefer or require them.

## Downstream handoff

`expand-procurement-market` may search broadly for large meeting-room displays and mounts consistent with the register.

`verify-procurement-options` must verify each candidate against hard minimums and separately report preferences/targets/unknown compatibility facts. A candidate must not be rejected solely for missing 4K, wireless casting, 75-inch exact size, or exceeding A$2,500 unless the user later promotes those states.

## Critical defects

- Turning 4K or A$2,500 into a mandatory minimum/cap.
- Turning wireless casting or 75-inch preference into mandatory.
- Requiring Samsung/LG.
- Inventing VESA dimensions or HDMI version.
- Inventing product features/standards not in source.
- Naming specific products or searching the market.
- Treating installation labour as in scope.
