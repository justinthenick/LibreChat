# Benchmark 025 Scoring Rubric — Evaluator Only

Score out of 100 before penalties.

## A. Domain and objective — 10

- 5: correctly classifies audiovisual/display hardware with mounting dependency.
- 5: states a vendor-neutral procurement objective preserving the existing dock/wall context.

## B. Hard minimum discipline — 25

- 5: one display / wall-mounted.
- 5: >=70-inch minimum.
- 5: existing HDMI-output compatibility.
- 5: fit within 1.85 m wall width.
- 5: does not introduce unsupported hard minimums.

## C. Preference/Target/Unknown preservation — 30

- 6: ~75-inch remains Preference.
- 6: wireless casting remains Preference.
- 6: 4K remains Target.
- 6: A$2,500 remains Target, not hard cap.
- 6: VESA/final mount details remain Unknown pending candidate selection.

## D. Scope and brand discipline — 15

- 5: brand remains open; Samsung/LG treated only as examples.
- 5: exclusions/out-of-scope items are preserved.
- 5: does not search or name products/vendors.

## E. Downstream handoff / verification evidence — 20

- 10: gives a useful market-expansion handoff that distinguishes hard vs preferred/target/unknown.
- 10: states evidence to obtain from candidate documentation without inventing validation methods or unsupported standards/specs.

## Critical penalties

- -30: hardens 4K or A$2,500 into a mandatory threshold/cap.
- -25: hardens 75-inch or wireless casting into mandatory.
- -25: invents VESA dimensions, HDMI version, Wi-Fi standard, panel/brightness/refresh/warranty/power/certification or similar unsupported product requirements.
- -20: requires or materially prefers Samsung/LG from the example references.
- -20: names/recommends specific products or performs market search.
- -15: brings installation labour or dock replacement back into scope.

## Decision guidance

- 90-100 with zero critical penalties: strong standalone candidate.
- 80-89 with zero critical penalties: retain for targeted correction/generalization.
- <80 or any critical penalty: revise before Architecture -> Procurement composition.
