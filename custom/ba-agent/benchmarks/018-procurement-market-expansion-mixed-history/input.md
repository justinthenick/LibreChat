# Benchmark 018 — Mixed-History Compact Treadmill Market Expansion

## Objective

Find a compact folding treadmill for a small Australian apartment.

The discovery task is **not** to verify or recommend a specific treadmill. It is to design the next market-search pass from the supplied search history without inventing live listings.

## Hard buying constraints that must remain intact

- Australian market and AUD pricing.
- Must be sold by an Australian supplier or already located in Australia; **no overseas import purchase**.
- Australian 230–240 V supply with a standard 10 A plug; no 110 V-only equipment or external step-down transformer workaround.
- Maximum purchase budget before optional accessories: **A$900 delivered**.
- Must support running speed of at least **12 km/h**.
- Must provide a genuine incline function; a walking pad with no incline is not acceptable.
- When folded/stored, longest dimension must be **no more than 150 cm** and widest dimension **no more than 80 cm**.
- User weight rating must be at least **110 kg**.
- New, refurbished, open-box and used are acceptable if the exact candidate can later be verified.

## Unsearched or lightly searched directions

No evidence is supplied that the following have been searched:

- specialist Australian fitness-equipment refurbishers or ex-demo outlets;
- Australian manufacturer/direct outlet or clearance channels;
- commercial gym-equipment liquidators selling compact/light-commercial units into the consumer market;
- local fitness-equipment service/repair businesses that resell refurbished units;
- Australian auction/liquidation channels;
- interstate Australian used stock outside the current Facebook/Gumtree regions;
- adjacent folding running-platform classes that still meet **all** speed, incline, footprint, voltage and weight-rating hard constraints.

## Tempting but invalid expansion directions

These may look like new coverage but violate hard constraints and should not be promoted merely for novelty:

- US fitness retailers shipping 110 V treadmills to Australia;
- Alibaba/AliExpress or other overseas-direct imports where the purchase would be an overseas import;
- non-incline walking pads, even if very compact and cheap;
- full-size commercial treadmills whose stored footprint exceeds the apartment limits;
- transformer-based workarounds for 110 V equipment.

## Important benchmark boundary

Do not browse the web and do not fabricate current listings, sellers, prices, stock or search results.

Your job is to decide **where and how to search next**, how much effort should exploit still-productive known channels versus explore genuinely new Australian channels, why that balance fits this mixed history, which tempting directions should be rejected, and when to stop or refresh.
