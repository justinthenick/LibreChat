# Benchmark 017 — Rental Kitchen Dishwasher Procurement Verification

## User objective

Buy a freestanding dishwasher for a rental kitchen. The appliance must fit the existing space and electrical supply without modifying the rental property.

The user wants a recommendation **only where the supplied evidence is strong enough to buy confidently**. Do not browse the web; assess only the evidence packet below.

## Requirements

### Hard gates

- **H-01 — Landed budget:** appliance plus stated mandatory freight must be no more than **A$900**.
- **H-02 — Capacity:** exact model/configuration must support at least **12 place settings**.
- **H-03 — Width:** appliance width must be **no more than 600 mm**.
- **H-04 — Height:** appliance height must be **no more than 850 mm**.
- **H-05 — Depth:** appliance depth must be **no more than 610 mm**.
- **H-06 — Electrical:** exact appliance must use a standard Australian **10 A, 230–240 V plug connection**. The rental does not permit hardwiring or a 15 A circuit change.

### Preferences

- **P-01:** inlet and drain hoses included.
- **P-02:** warranty or returns are preferred.
- **P-03:** lower landed cost is preferred after hard gates are satisfied.
- **P-04:** lower stated noise level is preferred, but there is no mandatory dB limit.

## Important benchmark boundary

Do not assume specifications not present in this packet. In particular:

- a product family containing a compliant model does not prove an ambiguous listing is that model;
- “standard 60 cm,” “normal depth” and “normal wall plug” are seller descriptions, not exact measurements or electrical-rating evidence;
- low price or low noise does not cure a failed or unresolved hard installation gate.
