# Benchmark 001 — Skill v0.3 Results (2026-09-01)

## Test setup

Benchmark: **001 — Change Validation Automation**

Model track: **Gemini 3.6 Flash**

- Baseline: same benchmark prompt/input with no skill.
- Skill runs: same benchmark prompt/input with `$analyze-requirements` v0.3.
- Gold standard and scoring rubric were not supplied to the model.
- Two independent v0.3 runs were completed to check repeatability.

## Repeatable strengths in v0.3

- Correctly separated Evidence class from Requirement status.
- Preserved tentative scope: top-ten change types remained Candidate rather than committed.
- Preserved six-week and two-minute statements as Targets rather than hard constraints/SLAs.
- Preserved blocking-vs-warning as Disputed.
- Correctly treated future remediation as Deferred.
- Produced strong source traceability and explicit unknowns.
- Repeatedly used **Decision owner: Unknown** where policy/governance authority was not established.
- Avoided invented CAB/vendor/API/retention/immutability requirements.
- Correctly assessed the material as Partially Ready for decomposition.

## Remaining minor failure modes

### 1. Proposed mechanism could leak into required decision/outcome

Examples included suggesting a technical spike, asynchronous handling, or a tiered enforcement model inside ambiguity/decision statements even though those mechanisms were not established by the source.

Expected treatment:

- Requirements/ambiguity section: state **what outcome must be decided or verified**.
- Analyst proposals: separately suggest **how** to discover, decide or implement it.

### 2. Activity could be confused with decision authority

The Platform Engineer was explicitly involved in feasibility investigation, but that does not establish ownership of a business, SLA or governance decision.

Expected treatment: distinguish evidenced activity/responsibility from established decision authority.

### 3. Modal wording should consistently match requirement status

Candidate/Target/Deferred items should not use mandatory modal verbs even when their status column is correct.

## Decision

**Retain v0.3 as validated and materially production-promising for the requirements-analysis stage.**

Create v0.4 as a small precision release to:

1. separate required outcomes from analyst-suggested mechanisms;
2. keep analyst-generated alternatives solely under Analyst proposals;
3. distinguish activity/responsibility from decision authority;
4. align requirement wording with Candidate/Target/Deferred status.

After v0.4, stop optimizing only against Benchmark 001 and introduce a materially different Benchmark 002 to test generalization rather than benchmark memorization/overfitting.
