## Strengths

The composite generalized from site-access work to a materially different service-governance problem without repeating the Benchmark 008 authority failure.

- Overall readiness is explicitly `Partially Ready`.
- Emergency approval remains disputed and `Decision Owner: Unknown`; no downstream emergency behavior is committed.
- Service Registry automation remains Candidate and is gated by a Spike/Discovery item.
- Finance Applications / Network Tools pilot scope remains Candidate and unapproved.
- One-business-day completion remains a non-binding Target.
- Quarterly recertification remains Deferred.
- Evidence retention remains Unknown and does not become a no-delete/no-purge rule.
- Process/governance constraints survive through acceptance criteria and assurance.
- Stable IDs and explicit handoffs preserve end-to-end traceability.
- No unsupported API/protocol/storage/queue/UI/test-tooling mechanisms or concrete test data are introduced.

## Deductions

1. **Stage 1 fidelity (-2):** `REQ-03` is worded as though the MIM approval proposition itself were the requirement (`MIM can approve`) rather than explicitly preserving both competing positions in the requirement description. Its status remains Disputed and Decision Owner Unknown, so this does not become a governance selection or penalty.
2. **Stage 4 conditional assurance (-4):** the security constraint (`CON-01`) is correctly preserved as conditional through Stage 2, but Stage 4 does not provide a conditional assurance state for approved service identity / least privilege / no new shared administrator account. The benchmark expected conditional security assurance coverage without committing Candidate automation.

## Architecture decision

**Freeze Composite BA Delivery Analyst v0.2 as the preferred BA architecture.**

Benchmark 008 scored 95/100 with zero penalties; Benchmark 009 scores 94/100 with zero penalties on a materially different domain. That is sufficient generalization evidence. Do not tune v0.2 against Benchmarks 008/009 for cosmetic gains.

The three-specialist pipeline remains experimental infrastructure, not the default architecture: on Benchmark 008 it scored 53/100 at 23,748 tokens and amplified upstream semantic errors downstream.

Next capability layer: **solution / change-readiness handoff**. Build and benchmark that as a separate capability while keeping the four retained BA skills and Composite v0.2 frozen unless a later independent benchmark reveals a genuine reusable defect.
