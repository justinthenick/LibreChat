## What improved

v0.3 fixes the reusable defect seen in v0.1/v0.2:

- no default list of invented UI/API/environment/test-data/tooling prerequisites;
- assurance checks state required conditions without prescribing inspection mechanisms;
- Ready criteria are fully covered and trace to AC, delivery-item and REQ IDs;
- failed-validation behaviour remains Blocked/Disputed rather than being converted into tests;
- Candidate import/pilot scope remain non-committed;
- the fifteen-minute Target remains non-binding;
- Deferred and Unknown areas remain outside committed testing.

## Minor residual wording

Two phrases remain slightly more prescriptive than necessary but do not create committed behaviour or execution design:

1. DEC-01 says further tests wait until the behaviour is defined **and a decision owner is established**. The source only establishes that the decision owner is currently Unknown; the owner should simply remain Unknown rather than becoming an independently required prerequisite.
2. OPEN-01 refers to future `data-purging` tests although no purge mechanism is established. The safe wording is simply retention/lifecycle coverage once the retention requirement is resolved.

Neither issue affects the actual committed test cases or conditional assurance checks, so no rubric penalty is applied.
