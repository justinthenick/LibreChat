## Finding

## Decision

Do not discard the capability. Revise to v0.2 with a tighter rule: remaining gaps may list only sourced unresolved inputs/decisions. Do not assert that UI/API/environment/test-data/sign-off mechanisms must exist when upstream does not establish them. Re-run Skill-only against Benchmark 007, retaining the clean baseline.
