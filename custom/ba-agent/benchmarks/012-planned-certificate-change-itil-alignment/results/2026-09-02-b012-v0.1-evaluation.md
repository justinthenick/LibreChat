## Findings

The skill generalizes well on most of the benchmark. It correctly:

- keeps `CHG-8526` Proposed / not authorised;
- does not promote `SCM-12` to cover the proxy-endpoint change;
- keeps the local Change Authority holder unidentified;
- identifies the schedule conflict and local policy requirement to resolve it before window finalisation;
- separates Change Enablement, Release Management, Deployment Management and Service Configuration Management;
- keeps production availability conditional on the change decision;
- preserves the engineer's restore-old-config/certificate suggestion as a proposal;
- explicitly rejects universal CAB and rollback-plan claims as ITIL mandates;
- avoids invented CMDB technology, security approval, PIR, maturity scores, APIs, workflows and execution mechanics.

## Reusable defect

Despite explicitly stating that neither ITIL nor supplied local policy universally mandates a rollback plan, Section 4 promotes an **agreed recovery/backout approach** to `Evidence Required`, and the follow-up questions ask for a formally agreed procedure as if it were a prerequisite for authorisation. The same section also promotes a configuration-update owner/timeline into a mandatory pre-authorisation planning gate rather than simply identifying those values as `Not evidenced` and asking whether local process requires them before implementation.

This is a narrower form of **gap-to-gate promotion**: a relevant practice concern or missing evidence category becomes a mandatory local readiness gate without source evidence that it is one.

No formal rubric penalty applies because the output does not claim a *universal ITIL* rollback mandate. However, the defect is governance-significant because it can make a change appear blocked by a requirement that the organisation never established.

## Decision

**Do not freeze v0.1 from B012 yet.** Make one focused correction only: `Not evidenced` practice concerns may be raised as questions or dependencies, but must not be labelled `Evidence required`, mandatory gates, approvals or pre-authorisation prerequisites unless explicit local policy/source evidence establishes that status. In particular, recovery/backout and configuration-update ownership/timing must not be promoted merely because they are relevant ITIL practice concerns.

Queue a Skill-only B012 rerun after that correction. Do not alter the frozen Composite BA Delivery Analyst while this isolated capability is being corrected.
