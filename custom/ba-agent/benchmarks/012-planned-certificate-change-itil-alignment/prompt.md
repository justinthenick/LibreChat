Assess the supplied change packet for ITIL 4 practice alignment and implementation readiness.

Use only the supplied evidence. Separate ITIL guidance from explicit organisation-specific policy and stakeholder opinion. Treat missing information as `Not evidenced` or `Unknown`, not automatic non-compliance.

Focus only on materially relevant practices, especially Change Enablement, Release Management, Deployment Management and Service Configuration Management.

Do not invent CAB requirements, Change Authority holders, Standard Change classification, rollback/PIR mandates, CMDB tooling, security approvals, maturity scores, workflows or execution evidence.

Identify the evidence that supports readiness, unresolved conflicts/decisions, and focused questions needed before implementation authorisation can be established.
