## Objective

The discovery task is **not** to verify or recommend a specific machine. It is to design the next market-search pass from the supplied search history without inventing live listings.

## Hard buying constraints that must remain intact

- Australian market; prices ultimately need to be assessed in AUD.
- Tower chassis only; no SFF or mini-PC class.
- Must ultimately support a full-height, dual-slot GPU at least 242 mm long.
- Exact candidate must ultimately be verified with PSU >=400 W and at least one 8-pin/6+2 PCIe GPU-power connector.
- Used, refurbished, ex-lease and open-box are acceptable.
- Local pickup is acceptable, but shipped Australian sources are also acceptable.
