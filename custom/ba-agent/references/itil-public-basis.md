# ITIL public reference basis

Reviewed: **2026-09-01**

This lab uses only high-level, publicly described ITIL 4 practice concepts and does not reproduce licensed Practice Guide content or claim to perform an official ITIL Maturity Model assessment.

Public PeopleCert reference pages used when creating `assess-itil-alignment` v0.1:

- ITIL 4 Practitioner: Change Enablement  
  https://www.peoplecert.org/browse-certifications/it-governance-and-service-management/ITIL-1/itil-4-practitioner-change-enablement-3794
- ITIL 4 Practitioner: Service Configuration Management  
  https://www.peoplecert.org/browse-certifications/it-governance-and-service-management/ITIL-1/itil-4-practitioner-service-configuration-management-3800
- ITIL 4 Specialist: Plan, Implement, and Control  
  https://www.peoplecert.org/browse-certifications/it-governance-and-service-management/ITIL-1/itil-4-specialist-plan-implement-and-control-3804

Public concepts used by the lab:

- Change Enablement concerns successful change through appropriate risk assessment, authorisation and change-schedule management/coordination.
- Service Configuration Management concerns accurate and reliable information about services/configuration items when and where needed.
- Plan, Implement and Control groups closely related practices including Change Enablement, Release Management, Service Configuration Management, Deployment Management and IT Asset Management.

Lab guardrails:

- ITIL guidance does not by itself establish an organisation's CAB requirement, Change Authority, Emergency Change Authority, rollback/PIR policy, configuration tooling or local workflow.
- Absence of evidence in a supplied packet is assessed as `Not evidenced`, not automatically `Non-compliant`.
- The lab does not assign an official ITIL maturity/capability score without authorised ITIL Maturity Model criteria.
